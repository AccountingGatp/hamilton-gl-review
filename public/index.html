GL review SOP

  1. Prepare the two Excel files. Export Trailing Profit and Loss Detail for the property with at least two months of actuals. In the GL heads workbook, put the account codes to monitor in column A or under a GL Account header.
  2. Upload and run the review. Choose the P&L export and the GL heads workbook in their respective fields. Select Run review.
  3. Confirm the comparison months. Check the property name and the two months shown. The dashboard starts with the latest two months in the report. Use Current month and Compare with to choose a different pair. Difference is the current month amount minus the comparison month amount.
  4. Review the results. Start with Selected GL heads for monitored accounts or switch to All P&L lines. Check any messages about unmatched codes or unreadable amounts. Clear Changes only to include lines with no change.
  5. Save the review. Select Download Excel to save the selected GL changes, all changes, full P&L review, and review notes for the chosen months.
